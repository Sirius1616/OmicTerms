# GHP / Innovation Stream — Cold Outreach (Canada)

Goal: introduce yourself to companies approved to hire Nigerian talent through Canada's Global Growth Stream / Innovation Stream (LMIA-exempt work permits, code C88). None of these have a matching Nigeria-open posting right now, so the play is direct, specific outreach to recruiting + applying to any relevant posting.

General rules:
- Send ONE message per company, to a real person (Talent Acquisition / Engineering Manager), not a generic inbox.
- Find the recruiter on LinkedIn: search `<company> Talent Acquisition` or `<company> recruiting`. Connect with a short note, or message within 48h of connecting.
- Attach the CV (John_Ezekiel_CV_Expedier.pdf works well — it's the generalist backend version; use Paystack one if you angle security).
- Do NOT mention "sponsorship" as a favour — reference the Innovation Stream factually, once.
- Follow up once after ~5 working days, then stop.

## Finding the right person (quick method)

1. LinkedIn → search `<company name>` → filter "People" → search `Talent Acquisition` or `Technical Recruiter`.
2. If the company lists a careers email in the posting, use it with the same body (subject: "Python Backend Developer — Nigeria — open to Innovation Stream role").
3. Alternatively use the careers portal's "join talent pool" option to get into their pipeline.
