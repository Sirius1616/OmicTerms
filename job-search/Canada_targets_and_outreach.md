# Canada — Verified Targets & Outreach (non-sponsor, individual companies that hire from Nigeria)

Reality check from the sweeps: publicly-posted Canadian roles are overwhelmingly tagged "Remote – Canada" and require Canadian work authorization (Autodesk, Tucows, Clario, Blackpoint, TaxGPT, Sardine, Guidewire, Slice, Lazer — all rejected for that reason). HostPapa says "work from anywhere" but its hiring focus is EU-only. So the viable Canada-from-Nigeria channel is individual Canadian companies that already hire Nigerian talent directly (proven by their workforce), not the job-board "Canada remote" listings.

Verified targets below. Ranked by likelihood of a real response.
